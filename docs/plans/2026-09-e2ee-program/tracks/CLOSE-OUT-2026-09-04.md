## The three defects that were not what they looked like

**The idle redial storm was never a client defect.** The client has reset its silence watchdog on any inbound frame since June. The streamer sent an idle client nothing JavaScript could observe — its only liveness signal was a WebSocket *protocol* ping, invisible below `onmessage`. `types.ts` declared the app-level frame and `ws-hub.ts` carried a comment describing it as "sealed here like every other frame". Nothing constructed one. **Someone wrote the guard rail and not the road**, and nothing detected it for months because the missing signal was invisible by construction.

**The continuation-frame row was unsatisfiable, not hard.** `ws-hub.ts` has one server-to-client send and passes no options; `ws@8.21.3` hardcodes `fin: true`. No payload size produces an opcode-0 frame, so the row as written would either never close or close on an absence meaning nothing. The gate was split rather than held: the capture half cleared R2, and the client-side fragmentation question moved to where it could actually be answered.

**Two nondeterministic tests were live in the streamer suite the whole time** and had nothing to do with this program. Both surfaced only because the program merged often enough to roll the dice repeatedly, and both live in tests whose *purpose* is proving nothing leaks — so each false red read as a security regression.

## The finding that outlives all of it

`tracks/D/PLAN-D.md` §14 now opens with a family rather than a list of traps: **an empty result that reads as an answer.** Eleven distinct mechanisms produced that one signature in two days.

In at least three, **the positive control failed identically to the check**, which is what made the emptiness look corroborated rather than broken. The refinement that carries the weight: *a positive control proves the instrument can answer, not that you asked it the right question.*

One member belonged to this program's own tooling, built two hours after the register describing that exact shape was written. Writing the catalogue did not prevent constructing a fresh instance of it.

## What the next session needs

`tracks/HANDOFF-2026-09-04.md` is the operational document. In short:

- **G-2 is one sitting** with both devices. The app is built and installed at versionCode 63 with its artefact verified in the served bundle; the method is settled; the runbook carries exact commands, the interface-derivation procedure, and the stale-entry step that made the last pairing fail at the first tap.
- **Capture permission is permanently solved** — `ChmodBPF` is installed and verified by an actual capture, including on a device node recreated after installation. The old manual `chgrp`/`chmod` instruction is obsolete and struck at the top of both the handover and the runbook.
- **R3 is deliberately unopened.** Its gate is G-2. A blanket instruction to proceed is not the explicit go in the user's own words that the flip requires.
- **One decision is the user's alone**: their real tunnel hostname is live on `main` in eleven files, and also in this repository's own instructions and deployment skills predating the program. No history was rewritten for it.
